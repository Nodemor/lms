# NodeMor Academy: user guide

The site works in **English, සිංහල and தமிழ்**. Switch at any time with the **EN / සිං / தமி** buttons at the top. Your choice is saved to your account and also used for your emails.

On any inner page, the **Back** button at the top returns to the previous page. On phones, the main sections are in the bar at the bottom of the screen.

---

## For teachers

### 1. Create your account
1. Click **Get started** and choose **I'm a teacher**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password (8+ characters with letters and numbers).
3. Enter the **6-digit code** sent to your email. Your dashboard opens immediately.
4. Go to **Settings** and add your headline, bio and **bank details**. Students see your bank details when paying by bank slip.

### 2. Create a class
1. Go to **My classes → New class**.
2. Fill in:
   - title, subject, grade and teaching language
   - schedule, fee type (free, monthly or one-time) and fee
   - who can join (anyone, or only students you approve)
   - optional seat limit and cover image
3. The class starts as a **draft**. Click **Publish class** when ready, then **Copy class link** and share it with students.

### 3. Run the class: the tabs

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| Overview | Edit class details, publish, archive or delete |
| Announcements | Post updates; pin important ones; students are emailed |
| Students | Approve or decline requests, add a student by email, record cash fees, export the list |
| Live classes | Schedule classes with a Zoom, Google Meet or Teams link; start; mark attendance |
| Lessons | Add recorded lessons from an unlisted YouTube link or by uploading a video, with description and lecture notes |
| Notes | Upload PDFs, Word files, slides; choose preview-only or downloadable |
| Assignments | Set work with a due date; mark submissions with feedback |
| Quizzes | Multiple-choice or true/false, with time limits; marked automatically |
| Attendance | Attendance percentage for each student; export to Excel |
| Payments | Confirm or reject bank slips, mark refunds, export to Excel |
| Progress | Each student's attendance, lessons watched and assignments submitted |

### 4. Live classes with Zoom, Google Meet or Teams
1. Go to **Live classes → Schedule live class**.
2. Enter the topic, time and length, and paste your meeting link. Next time, your last link is filled in for you.
3. Students are notified, and reminded 30 minutes before.
4. Students see a **Join now** button 15 minutes before the start. It takes them straight to your meeting, and only if they're enrolled and have paid.
5. Click **Start class** to open your meeting.
6. After class, click **Mark class as ended**, open **Attendance**, and tick **Present** or **Absent** for each student. The list shows who clicked Join and when.

Turn on a passcode or waiting room in your meeting app for extra safety.

### 5. Collecting fees
- **Bank slip:** the student uploads a slip, and you open **Payments**, view the slip, and click **Confirm** or **Reject** (with a reason).
- **Cash:** in **Students**, click **Record cash payment** next to the student.

For monthly classes, a student's content locks until that month's fee is paid.

### 6. Your package
**My plan** shows your limits and usage. To upgrade, tap **Request on WhatsApp** next to a package (WhatsApp 071 924 7009, or email nodemoracademy@gmail.com). We send the bank details, and after payment we activate the package. Nothing is deleted if a package expires; you just return to the Free limits.

### 7. QR codes
- **Class QR:** in your class **Overview**, use **Download QR** or **Print poster**. Students scan it to see the class and your mentor details, and join.
- **In-person classes:** when scheduling, choose **In person (QR check-in)**. At class time, tap **Show check-in QR** and show it on a projector, TV or tablet at the door. Students scan it with their phone camera and are marked present. The code changes every 30 seconds, so a photo sent to someone at home won't work.

## For students

1. **Sign up:** click **Get started**, choose **I'm a student**, and enter the email code.
2. **Find a class:** use **Find classes** (filter by subject or language) or open the link your teacher sent, then click **Join class**. If the teacher approves each request, you're notified when they do.
3. **Pay the fee:** open the class, go to the **Fees** tab, pay to the bank account shown, choose the month, upload a photo of your slip and send it. Your teacher confirms it and the class opens. Questions? Tap the WhatsApp button on the Fees page.
4. **In-person classes:** scan the QR code your teacher shows at the class to check in.
5. **Learn:**
   - **Live classes:** click **Join now** when it appears.
   - **Lessons:** watch the video, open the lecture notes, and continue where you stopped.
   - **Notes:** open or download files.
   - **Assignments:** write your answer or attach a file, then submit.
   - **Quizzes:** answers save automatically, and the quiz submits itself when time runs out.
   - **Announcements:** read your teacher's updates.
6. **Check your progress** in **My results** (all marks; export to Excel) and in each class's **Attendance** tab.
